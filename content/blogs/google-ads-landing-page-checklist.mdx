Paid clicks cost money, so every problem on the landing page is paid for twice: once for the click and once for the lost visitor. This is the checklist I run before a page goes live. It comes from building [200+ PPC landing pages](/blog/ppc-landing-page-conversion-lessons), and it is deliberately boring: most failed pages miss something on this list rather than something clever.

## Message

**1. Message match.** The headline repeats what the ad promised. If the ad says "free quote in 24 hours", the first screen says so.

**2. One goal.** One primary action per page. Repeat that same action, do not add a second one.

**3. The first screen answers "is this for me?"** Who it is for, what they get and what to do next, visible without scrolling on a phone.

**4. No site navigation.** A logo is enough. Menus give paid visitors exits.

## Trust

**5. Real proof only.** Real logos, real quotes or real numbers you can back up. If you have none yet, say less rather than inventing it. A modest honest page converts better than a suspicious one.

**6. Plain contact details and policies.** Privacy policy linked, a real way to reach you, and no surprises after the click.

## Form

**7. Ask for the minimum.** A cold ad click will give a name and email far more readily than a phone number, company size and project brief. Ask for more later.

**8. Make errors obvious.** Visible labels, clear error messages and a submit button that says what happens next. Do not rely on placeholder text alone.

## Speed and mobile

**9. Fast on a real phone.** Test on mobile, not just your desktop. Target LCP under 2.5 seconds, INP under 200 milliseconds and CLS under 0.1. The usual culprits and fixes are in [why a website is slow on mobile](/blog/why-is-my-website-slow-on-mobile).

**10. Thumb-friendly.** Large tap targets, readable text without zooming, and the main button reachable on the first screen at about 375 by 667 pixels.

## Tracking

**11. Tag the traffic.** Use consistent UTM parameters on every ad URL. The [UTM Builder](/lab/utm-builder) keeps existing parameters and flags a missing `utm_` prefix.

**12. Test the whole conversion.** Submit the form yourself, confirm it arrives, confirm the conversion shows in your ad platform, and confirm the thank-you state works. Do this before spending anything.

## Common failure: fixing the page before the traffic

Do not redesign a page on a hunch. Check the list above first, then change one thing at a time so you know what helped.
